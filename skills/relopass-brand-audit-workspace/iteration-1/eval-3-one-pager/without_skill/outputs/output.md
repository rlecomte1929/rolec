# ReloPass One-Pager Evaluation

## Overall Assessment
This one-pager is **functional but needs refinement**. It covers the key message pillars but lacks persuasion, differentiation, and emotional resonance that converts prospects in post-demo moments.

---

## What Works Well

**1. Clear Structure**
- The hierarchy (What, How, Why, CTA) is logical and easy to follow
- Appropriate for a leave-behind that complements a demo

**2. Target Audience Clarity**
- "HR teams" signals exactly who should care
- "One place" resonates with the coordination pain point

**3. Simple CTA**
- Single email contact is unambiguous and action-oriented

---

## Critical Issues

### 1. **Weak Value Proposition**
The opening doesn't answer "Why should I care?" in emotional or business terms:
- "Helps HR teams coordinate relocations across vendors, employees, and documents" is feature-focused, not benefit-focused
- Missing the underlying pain: scattered tools, compliance risk, employee frustration, high touch labor

**Better approach:** Lead with the problem solved or outcome achieved
- Current: "all-in-one relocation management platform"
- Stronger: "Stop managing relocations across 5+ different tools"

### 2. **"Real relocations, not demos" Is Confusing**
This bullet is unclear and self-referential:
- Assumes the reader knows what ReloPass was demoing (they just saw it)
- Reads defensive rather than confident
- Doesn't communicate actual value

**Fix:** Replace with a concrete outcome metric or use case
- "Handles 100+ relocations per year without manual coordination"
- "From first contact to final relocation in 30% less time"

### 3. **"How It Works" Section Is Too Generic**
Three steps that could apply to almost any SaaS:
1. Onboard your team
2. Add your relocations
3. Manage everything in one place

These are process steps, not value indicators. A prospect won't remember these.

**Better approach:** Show the transformation
- "Centralize all vendor communication and documents"
- "Track every relocation from start to finish with real-time visibility"
- "Eliminate spreadsheets and email chains"

### 4. **Missing Social Proof or Credibility**
Post-demo is when trust matters most:
- No numbers (# companies, relocations handled, time saved)
- No customer names or industry context
- No third-party validation

Even one line would help:
- "Trusted by 200+ HR teams managing 10,000+ relocations annually"

### 5. **Vague Differentiators**
"Best-in-class vendor network" needs explanation:
- What vendors? (Movers, housing, immigration, etc.)
- Why does this matter? (Cost savings, reduced employee friction, compliance)
- How is it "best-in-class"?

---

## Copy Tone & Brand Issues

**Current tone:** Product-focused, generic
**Should be:** Empathetic, confident, transformation-focused

- "Our powerful tools" sounds generic (all SaaS say this)
- "Keep everyone in the loop" is weak—no sense of urgency or benefit
- Missing any acknowledgment of the relocation experience from the employee perspective

---

## Recommended Changes (Priority Order)

### High Priority
1. **Rewrite the opening** to lead with pain or outcome, not features
2. **Replace "Real relocations, not demos"** with concrete value (speed, cost, compliance, employee satisfaction)
3. **Rebuild "How It Works"** to show transformation, not process steps

### Medium Priority
4. **Add a credibility line** (customer count or relocation volume)
5. **Expand "Best-in-class vendor network"** with context about why it matters

### Nice to Have
6. **Add a secondary CTA** (e.g., "Schedule a 15-minute walkthrough") for those not ready to email
7. **Include a subtle visual element** (icon, graph, or stat) to break up text

## Final Verdict

**Current version:** 5/10 (serviceable, but forgettable)
**Revised version potential:** 8/10 (persuasive, credible, memorable)

The one-pager has the right structure but reads like product documentation, not a sales tool. Post-demo, prospects need to remember *why* ReloPass solves their specific problem—not just *what* it does. The revision above focuses on outcomes, credibility, and emotional resonance.
