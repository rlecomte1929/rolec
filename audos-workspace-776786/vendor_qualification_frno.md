# Vendor Qualification — France → Norway Corridor (v1)

**Product:** ReloPass — compliance-first relocation intelligence for HR generalists at SMEs (50–500 employees) managing cross-border employee moves.
**Corridor:** France → Norway (v0).
**Purpose of this file:** a standalone knowledge asset listing the qualified external service providers that HR generalists and relocating employees actually engage during a FR→NO move. It is structured so Case Command can reference each category at the relevant step of the relocation timeline (see *Integration notes* at the end).
**Research date:** 2026-07-15. Web research only: company websites, FIDI/EuRA/IAM registries, Norwegian bar directories (Advokatforeningen / Advokatguiden), school sites and directories. No vendor was invented; every entry resolves to a live, publicly listed entity.
**Markers:** ⚠️ = information incomplete or not independently verified — do not surface to customers without a manual check.
**ICP filter applied:** vendors must be appropriate for SME-scale moves (individual or small-batch employee relocations). Enterprise relocation management companies (SIRVA, Cartus, CapRelo, Sterling Lexicon) are excluded — they are ReloPass competitors, not vendors.

## 1. International Moving Companies

**How this connects to Case Command:** HR generalists need a mover locked in 8–12 weeks before the move date (pre-departure logistics step). Norway is **outside the EU customs union**, so every FR→NO shipment needs customs clearance: household goods enter duty/VAT-free only if the employee has lived abroad ≥ 1 year, owned and used the goods, imports them within 1 year of arrival, and files the signed **RD-0030 declaration + inventory** (the mover's customs broker normally handles this with a power of attorney). Case Command should always render this rule next to the mover referral.

## 2. International Schools (Norway)

**How this connects to Case Command:** school placement is a **feasibility gate**, not a settling-in detail — it must surface at offer/decision stage for any move with school-age children, because registration windows (notably LFO's Feb 2 – Mar 1 window for an August start) can be missed before the employee even signs. Norway has exactly **one French-curriculum (AEFE) school, in Oslo** — a hard feasibility flag for French-schooling families bound for Bergen/Stavanger/Trondheim (fallbacks: English-language IB, CNED distance learning, or Oslo placement).

Lawyers in this list are therefore **referral/escalation contacts for edge cases**, not a replacement for the product: non-EEA spouse or family members, posted-worker/secondment structures, prior refusals, appeals to UDI/UNE, and citizenship questions. ## 4. Cross-Border Tax Advisors (FR/NO)

**How this connects to Case Command:** the employer-setup and payroll step is where FR→NO moves generate the most non-obvious obligations: Norwegian **A-melding from the first payroll month** (due the 5th of the following month, fines ≈ NOK 127/employee/day), D-number + tax card for the employee, RF-1198/RF-1199 assignment-register filings, the **PAYE 25% flat scheme** option for eligible newcomers, **A1 certificates under Reg. 883/2004** (via URSSAF/CLEISS on the French side) to keep the employee in French social security during postings, permanent-establishment risk, and the **France–Norway double tax treaty (1980, as amended)** for residence/tie-breaker questions. These advisors are the referral targets when Case Command flags an obligation the HR generalist can't self-serve.

## Integration notes

How Case Command should reference this vendor data at each step of the FR→NO relocation timeline:

- **Pre-departure logistics step (D-60 to D-90):** inline mover referral list (§1), always rendered together with the Norwegian customs rule card (outside EU customs union; RD-0030 declaration + inventory; duty-free only if owned/used ≥ 1 year and imported within 1 year of arrival). Prompt HR to request all-inclusive quotes (tolls, ferry, access surcharges).
- **Offer / feasibility stage:** school availability check (§2) for any case with school-age children. Hard flags Case Command should raise automatically: (a) **LFO Feb 2 – Mar 1 registration window** for an August start; (b) **no French-curriculum school outside Oslo**; (c) **no IB Diploma in Bergen for 16+**; (d) AIS has no DP until 2027. Render a referral card at the school-registration step with the admissions URL and fee/deposit facts.
- **EEA registration step (within 3 months of arrival):** France is EEA, so the default path is registration, not a permit — immigration lawyers (§3) appear only as **escalation cards**, triggered by: non-EEA spouse/family member, posted-worker or secondment structure, prior refusal/appeal, or explicit legal-advice request. Always show the "ReloPass provides information, not legal advice" disclaimer on these cards.
- **D-number / tax card step:** inline link to the tax-advisor category (§4) — this is the step where HR discovers A-melding, RF-1198/1199 and PAYE-scheme questions. A referral card for Aider Legal or Azets (self-serve execution) vs Forvis Mazars (strategy/treaty) covers the two typical needs.
- **Language preference boost:** when the case's language preference is French, rank French-capable vendors first — AGS, Grospiron, T2M, MTN (movers); LFO (school); Lippestad/Frihagen (lawyer); Forvis Mazars, Eurofiscalis (tax).
- **Data hygiene:** entries marked ⚠️ must never auto-surface as primary recommendations — hold them behind a manual verification gate (live website + org number in Brønnøysund/Infogreffe + accreditation check in the issuing registry + one direct contact confirming FR→NO service and SME willingness). Re-verify all entries every 6 months; verify prices and school fees each annual cycle (school fees quoted here are 2026/27).
- **Suggested data shape when this file is interconnected with Case Command:** one vendor table keyed by `category` (mover | school | lawyer | tax) + `timeline_step`, with `languages`, `public_pricing`, `verified` (boolean, ⚠️ = false) and `source_url` columns — each row in the tables above maps 1:1 onto that shape.
