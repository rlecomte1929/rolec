**Purpose:** extends the city-indexed vendor research beyond the FR→NO v0 corridor to the main cities of four roadmap countries — **Germany (Berlin, Munich, Frankfurt), United Kingdom (London, Manchester), India (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bangalore), Japan (Tokyo, Osaka/Kansai)** — with a minimum of 5 providers per city in the same import/export-ready table shape as `vendor_directory_by_city_frno.md`.
**Research date:** 2026-07-17. Movers were checked against the FIDI/FAIM affiliate registry or their own accreditation pages during this pass; schools and professional firms are established institutions verified as live entities — corridor-level facts (fees, deadlines, current accreditation) must still clear the standard manual gate before customer-facing use.
**Markers:** ✓ = existence, city and category confirmed (FIDI registry or provider site checked this pass). ⚠️ = live entity but a key fact (local branch, accreditation, current status) not independently confirmed — verify before referral.
**ICP filter (unchanged):** enterprise relocation management companies are excluded as ReloPass competitors (SIRVA, Cartus, CapRelo, Sterling Lexicon, Santa Fe, Crown World Mobility; Movinga-style brokers listed only with caution). These cities correspond to the demo/roadmap corridors already in the platform (e.g. Mumbai→Berlin, Singapore→London, Tokyo→Sydney) — **none of this content is lawyer-assured; it is authoring-stage research only.**

## Usage notes

- **These cities are roadmap research, not sellable corridors.** They align with the demo cases already in the platform (Mumbai→Berlin, Singapore→London, Tokyo→Sydney, etc.) and give Case Command a credible vendor long-list per destination — but nothing here has corridor rules or lawyer assurance behind it yet.
- **Same data hygiene as FR→NO:** ⚠️ entries never auto-surface; manual gate = live site + local company-register check + accreditation registry + one direct contact confirming the route and SME willingness. Re-verify every 6 months.
- **French-preference boost carries over:** every city section includes the local AEFE French school where one exists (Berlin, Munich, Frankfurt, London, Delhi, Tokyo).
- **Import path:** identical column set to `vendor_directory_by_city_frno.md` — the two files can be concatenated and imported into the WorkspaceDB `vendors` table in a single pass (`Provider→name`, `Category→service_type`, `Coverage→city/country`, `Website→website`, `Verified→verified`, `Notes→notes`).
