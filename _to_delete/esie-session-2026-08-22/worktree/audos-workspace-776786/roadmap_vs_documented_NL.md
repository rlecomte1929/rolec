# Roadmap vs Documented — Netherlands Immigration
## ReloPass NL Knowledge Base | Research Date: August 2026

All roadmaps use two representative nationalities:
- **EU representative:** Germany (DE) — illustrates free movement route
- **Non-EU representative:** India (IN) — illustrates IND permit routes

## Nationality-Specific Route Deviations

| Nationality | Route Variation |
|-------------|----------------|
| PL, DE, IT, ES, RO, BG, FR, BE, PT, GR | EU free movement; no IND permit; gemeente registration only |
| GB | Post-Brexit non-EU; follows same IND route as IN, US, CN |
| UA | Temporary Protection Directive (check current status); regular routes also available |
| SY, IR | Regular IND routes available; asylum route is separate and not covered here |
| TR | Non-EU; regular IND routes; note EU-Turkey Association Agreement may affect some fees |
