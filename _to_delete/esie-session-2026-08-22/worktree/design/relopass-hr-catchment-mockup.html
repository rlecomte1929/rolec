What the employee sees

Neighbours are on by default for origin-sourced categories, so the Strasbourg case works with zero HR action. Two guards keep that from becoming noise: short-sea and cross-bloc pairs are excluded via country_adjacency.default_included, and a neighbour with zero eligible providers is never rendered at all — Belgium, Italy and Luxembourg are absent above, not greyed out.

Three tiers resolve the catchment: corridor override → company policy → system default. “Apply to all routes” writes the company policy, so the choice is made once rather than per corridor — including the opt-out, for a company that wants origin-country providers only. Counts everywhere reflect the full eligibility check — in catchment and able to reach the destination — not just providers present in that country.

Why add Germany?

This is recorded on every vendor you approve from there, so the choice reads as deliberate in the audit trail.
