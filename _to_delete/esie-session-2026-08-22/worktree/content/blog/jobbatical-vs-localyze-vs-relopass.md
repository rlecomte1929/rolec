# Jobbatical vs Localyze vs ReloPass: SME Mobility Software Comparison (2026)

If you're evaluating mobility software for a company running 5–100 relocations per year, you've probably run into Jobbatical and Localyze in your research. Both are well-regarded. Both solve real problems. And both are meaningfully different from each other — and from ReloPass.

This comparison is honest. Each platform has genuine strengths and real limitations. The right choice depends on what your primary problem actually is.

## Jobbatical

### What it is

Jobbatical started as a talent marketplace and pivoted into immigration services. Its core offering today is managed immigration: you hand a case to Jobbatical, and their team — which includes in-house immigration specialists — manages the permit application process on your behalf.

### Strengths

**Full-service immigration management.** If you don't have internal immigration expertise and don't want to build it, Jobbatical removes that problem. Their specialists handle the filing, the document collection, the embassy coordination, and the follow-up. HR's involvement is limited to providing access and approvals.

**Strong for complex cases.** For difficult permit categories, unusual nationalities, or routes with high compliance risk, having a specialist team on the case reduces the chance of errors that restart the clock.

**Employee experience.** Jobbatical invests in the relocating employee's experience, not just the administrative process. There's guidance for the employee on what to expect at each stage.

### Limitations

**Cost scales with volume.** Managed services are priced per case, and the all-in cost per relocation is significantly higher than a platform-only model. For companies doing 20+ relocations per year, the per-case cost becomes a material line item.

**Limited HR visibility.** Because Jobbatical manages the case, HR doesn't have granular real-time visibility into what's happening. You know the status, not the detail.

**No policy enforcement layer.** Jobbatical manages immigration, but it doesn't enforce your company's relocation policy — which benefits are available, what the approval workflow is, what compliance steps are required beyond the permit. That still lives in your HR team's processes (or lack thereof).

**HRIS integration is light.** Case creation is largely manual; there's no deep bidirectional sync with Personio or BambooHR.

### Who it's right for

Companies where HR Ops doesn't have immigration expertise, compliance risk tolerance is low, volume is moderate (5–20 cases per year), and budget allows for full-service pricing. Also strong for companies with complex, non-standard routes where specialist oversight matters more than cost efficiency.

## Localyze

### What it is

Localyze is a relocation process platform that combines case management, employee-facing tools, and partner network access. It's built for HR teams who want to run their relocation process through a structured workflow rather than outsourcing it entirely.

### Strengths

**End-to-end workflow.** Localyze covers the relocation from permit to housing to settling-in support. For companies that want a single platform covering all relocation phases, it's the most comprehensive option in this comparison.

**Employee-facing tools.** Localyze has strong employee-facing functionality — employees have their own portal where they can see their checklist, upload documents, and track progress. This reduces the back-and-forth between HR and the employee.

**HRIS integration.** Localyze integrates with select HRIS platforms, enabling case creation from HRIS events.

**Partner network.** Localyze has a built-in network of immigration lawyers, relocation companies, and housing providers that can be engaged through the platform.

### Limitations

**Priced for mid-market.** Localyze's platform fee and per-case pricing is designed for companies doing significant relocation volume. For companies running fewer than 20 cases per year, the economics are less favourable.

**Complexity of setup.** The breadth of the platform is a strength for large teams; it can be overhead for small HR teams who just need case management and compliance tracking.

**Policy enforcement is partial.** Localyze has configurable workflows but policy enforcement — ensuring your company's specific rules are applied consistently to every case — is less granular than purpose-built policy engines.

**Immigration is via partners, not in-house.** Unlike Jobbatical, Localyze doesn't have in-house immigration specialists. Immigration support is sourced through their partner network, which works well for standard cases but adds a coordination layer for complex ones.

### Who it's right for

Mid-market companies (200–2,000 employees) running 20–100 relocations per year who want a comprehensive end-to-end platform, have the operational bandwidth to configure and manage it, and want to keep relocation process in-house rather than outsourcing it. Strong fit when employee experience and self-service are priorities.

## ReloPass

### What it is

ReloPass is a relocation coordination and compliance platform built specifically for HR teams at companies running 5–100 relocations per year. It's the coordination layer between your HRIS, your providers, and your HR team — not a managed service.

### Strengths

**HRIS-native.** Bidirectional sync with Personio and BambooHR: new hires marked as relocating automatically create draft cases; case milestones sync back to the employee record. This eliminates the manual data entry that causes most early-stage case errors.

**Policy enforcement.** ReloPass lets you configure your company's relocation policy — which benefits apply to which employee tier, which compliance steps are required for which routes, which providers are approved — and enforces it automatically across every case. No more inconsistency between cases managed by different HR team members.

**Compliance-first design.** The platform tracks permit expiry dates, A1 certificate renewals, registration deadlines, and posted worker obligations — and surfaces them before they become problems. The default assumption is that missing a deadline is costly.

**Provider coordination without email.** Immigration lawyers, relocation companies, and housing providers each get a task portal scoped to their cases. HR assigns tasks; providers respond in the platform; there's a complete audit trail.

**Priced for SME volume.** The pricing model is designed for companies running 5–100 cases per year — not for enterprise volumes that would make a platform fee appropriate.

### Limitations

**No managed immigration service.** ReloPass coordinates your immigration providers; it doesn't replace them. If you want someone else to manage the permit application, you still need an immigration lawyer or managed service provider. ReloPass helps you coordinate that relationship, not substitute for it.

**Not the right fit for very large volumes.** For companies running 200+ relocations per year, the feature set — while well-matched to SME needs — may not cover all the reporting and analytics requirements of a large mobility team.

**Fewer corridors with pre-built content.** Localyze has a broader library of country-specific guidance built into the platform. ReloPass covers the most common EU corridors thoroughly and is expanding.

### Who it's right for

Companies with 50–500 employees running 5–100 relocations per year who want operational control over their relocation process, need HRIS integration to eliminate manual case creation, have existing provider relationships they want to keep, and prioritise compliance tracking over outsourced case management.

## Decision Guide

**Choose Jobbatical if:** You want to hand off immigration management entirely, don't have internal expertise, and are willing to pay full-service pricing for the risk reduction.

**Choose Localyze if:** You're at 200+ employees, running significant relocation volume, want an end-to-end platform including employee self-service, and have the operational bandwidth to configure a comprehensive workflow.

**Choose ReloPass if:** You want to keep your process in-house, need your HRIS to be the trigger for case creation, have existing provider relationships, and want compliance tracking that prevents permit expiry and missed deadlines without outsourcing the work.
