# Competitive brief — ReloPass vs. Topia Horizon

**Audience:** ReloPass sales, for prospects who compare us to Topia Horizon. **Status:** internal draft —
Romain to review before any external use. **Last updated:** 2026-07-05 (AIQ-1412).

**Sources (verify before quoting):** Topia's Horizon launch release (PRNewswire, "Topia launches Horizon…",
302739509) and [topia.com](https://www.topia.com). Note: **topia.io is a different company** (a
virtual-world SaaS) — the mobility Topia is **topia.com**. **Topia publishes no pricing** — see the pricing note.

---

## The one-line take
**Topia is a mature, enterprise-grade mobility *console* for the mobility team. ReloPass is an AI-native,
*employee-first* mobility platform built for mid-market speed** — same Horizon-class AI (natural-language
policy config, guided AI journeys, grounded immigration answers), without the enterprise weight, long
onboarding, or Fortune-100 footprint — and with the relocating *employee*, not just the mobility team, as a
first-class user.

## 1. What Topia shipped (Horizon)
Topia (founded 2010; customers incl. ASML, Dell, Novartis, AXA, H&M, Schneider Electric) is an established,
software-only enterprise mobility platform (190+ countries; relocation cost/balance-sheets, tax, immigration,
business-travel & remote-work compliance, duty of care, vendor coordination). In 2026 it launched **Horizon**,
its "agentic AI platform for global mobility," adding:
- **Agentic AI throughout** — proactively surfaces insights, automates tasks, suggests next actions in-workflow.
- **Natural-language policy builder** — "describe your program in plain English → fully configured, audit-ready
  policies instantly."
- **Compliance intelligence** — country-by-country tracking, proactive alerts, risk scoring, audit-ready docs.
- **AI-assisted cost modeling** — estimates across comp, tax, housing, relocation *before* a move is approved.
- **MCP-native** integration; positioning: *"AI-native, not AI-bolted-on … no implementation consultants, no
  months-long setup."* (No quantified metrics and no pricing were published.)

The important read: Horizon is a strong, credible product — and several of its headline features (NL policy
builder especially) are things ReloPass also builds. So we **cannot** win on "we have AI too." We win on *who
the product is for* and *how fast it lands*.

## 2. Where ReloPass wins (specific, defensible)
- **Employee-first, not team-first.** ReloPass makes the *relocating employee* a first-class user with a guided
  journey (intake → services & policy → roadmap). Topia's employee experience is "clear guidance and real-time
  status" — the operator (the mobility team) is still the primary user. If the buyer cares about the *employee's*
  experience end-to-end, that's our home turf.
- **Horizon-class AI, mid-market fit.** We match the standout capabilities — natural-language policy
  configuration, AI-guided journeys, and **grounded, citation-backed immigration answers** — but built for smaller
  mobility teams that want value in days, not an enterprise rollout across 190 countries.
- **Transparency by design.** ReloPass shows honest "not tracked yet / confirm with the authority" states rather
  than fabricated confidence — buyers trust exactly what they see, and provenance is explicit. It's a real
  product principle, not a slogan.
- **Auditable AI answers.** Our immigration guidance is retrieval-grounded and cites its sources, so an HR user
  can check the basis of an answer — useful in a compliance-sensitive buy.

## 3. Where Topia wins (be honest)
- **Maturity & enterprise trust.** ~16 years, Fortune-100 logos, a 2025 recapitalization. ReloPass is early —
  don't fight Topia head-to-head for a global-enterprise, 190-country program.
- **Breadth & depth.** Tax compliance, cost balance sheets, duty of care, business-travel/remote-work compliance,
  and deep country coverage exceed ReloPass's current scope.
- **Cost modeling.** Topia has real cost estimates/balance sheets *and* Horizon's AI cost modeling. **ReloPass
  does not track per-case spend yet** — this is a genuine gap; don't claim cost superiority.
- **Compliance at scale.** Topia's country-by-country compliance + proactive alerts are mature and populated;
  ReloPass's equivalents are earlier-stage.

## 4. Recommended positioning statement
> "Topia Horizon is excellent if you're a global enterprise running a 190-country program with a dedicated
> mobility team. ReloPass gives mid-market companies the same AI-native experience — natural-language policy
> setup, AI-guided employee journeys, grounded immigration answers — designed around the *employee*, live in
> days, without enterprise onboarding or an enterprise footprint. If your relocating people (not just your HR
> team) are the ones who need a great experience, that's what we build for."

## 5. Three questions to ask a prospect who mentions Topia
1. **"Will your relocating employees actually log in and self-serve their move in Topia — or is it mainly your
   mobility team's console?"** (Surfaces the employee-first gap; Topia's employee side is status/guidance, not a
   driven journey.)
2. **"How long is Topia's onboarding for *your* program and corridors, and does it need their implementation
   team?"** (Time-to-value; even with Horizon's "no consultants" claim, deploying the broader platform varies —
   we land faster for mid-market.)
3. **"What has Topia quoted you for your headcount and country mix?"** (Pricing is quote-based/not public — so we
   *ask* rather than assume. It surfaces enterprise pricing that mid-market often finds heavy, and gives us a real
   number to respond to honestly.)

---

### Pricing note (mandatory honesty guardrail)
Topia does **not** publish pricing (quote-based, enterprise sales). **Do not make head-to-head cost or "X%
cheaper" claims** until a prospect shares an actual Topia quote. Compete on fit, speed-to-value, and the
employee experience — not on invented numbers.

### Internal caveat
This is competitive *positioning* for sales, not a shipped-feature audit. Some ReloPass capabilities referenced
(e.g. the NL policy builder, mobility-coordinator agent, risk dashboard) are behind rollout flags or are
early-stage; confirm current shipped state before making a specific live-feature claim to a prospect.
