Human oversight — a person decides, every time

ReloPass AI never acts on its own. It surfaces a recommendation; a named HR or admin user records an explicit decision (override and reject require a written reason). The decision is stored next to the exact AI output behind it — the Article 14 evidence high-risk HR systems must keep.

1
AI recommends — a policy insight, exception assessment, or provider/corridor match, with its reasoning and source policy shown.
2
A human reviews — a named reviewer reads it in context. Nothing is auto-applied.
3
The decision is logged — accept / override / reject, plus reviewer, timestamp, and the original AI output, in an immutable record.
4
Auditors can replay it — every AI-assisted decision is queryable and exportable on demand.

Transparency, data & record-keeping

  • Cited, not invented — the policy assistant answers only from your published policy and shows the source for each claim; if a question isn’t covered, it says so.
  • Purpose-bound, EU-hosted — personal data is processed to run the case it belongs to, scoped to the employee’s company, on EU-region infrastructure with row-level tenant isolation.
  • A regulator-ready trail — decisions, document changes, and AI-assisted actions are recorded with actor, timestamp, and the data they touched.
