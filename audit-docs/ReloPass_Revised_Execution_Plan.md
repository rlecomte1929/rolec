## Problem Statement

The original implementation plan was written without codebase access, so it estimated effort from first principles and assumed worst-case scenarios (audit log rebuild, FX build from scratch, Employee Dashboard as net-new work). A codebase inspection reveals that 3-4 of the plan's largest effort estimates are off because significant foundations already exist. This revised plan exploits what's already built to reach a demable mid-market product in ~3 weeks instead of 12, then layers in depth through week 10.

**Target milestone:** Mid-market prospect confidence — an HR lead at a 500-person company sees a live demo and says "this is real."  
**Definition of done for Sprint 1:** Spike 5 audit coverage verified + T1.5 v1 Estimate Review live + quick wins shipped.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A — Plan as written, slightly compressed
Trust the original sequencing. Accept 3-5 weeks for T1.5. Fix Spike 5 as a prerequisite.  
**Effort:** L (12-17 weeks) | **Risk:** Medium | **Drops speed on the table.**

### Approach B — Front-load the demo, layer in depth *(chosen)*
Sprint 1 exploits what's already built to ship a demable product in 3-4 weeks. Sprint 2 layers in the depth (ExceptionRequest, HR-side estimate view, tenant thresholds).  
**Effort:** M (3-4 weeks to first demo, 10 weeks to complete Tier 1) | **Risk:** Low

### Approach C — Demo-first, skip Spike 5
Ship estimate skeleton and quick wins in week 1-2. Demo immediately. Harden after.  
**Effort:** S to demo, unknown total | **Risk:** High — unverified tenant isolation before prospect demo unacceptable for HR data SaaS.

## Recommended Approach — Approach B: Two-Sprint Sequence

### Sprint 1 — Weeks 1-3: Demo-Ready

**Goal:** A working demo: HR sees estimate review with policy caps, employee sees their journey, audit coverage verified.

#### Week 1 (parallel tracks)

**Track A — Spike 5: Audit coverage verification + gap fill**  
*1-2 weeks. Do this first. It's not existential — it's a coverage sprint.*

What to do:
1. Read all 13 router files that lack `insert_audit_log`: `cases.py`, `admin.py`, `admin_collaboration.py`, `admin_freshness.py`, `admin_notifications.py`, `admin_ops_analytics.py`, `admin_review_queue.py`, `admin_staging.py`, `admin_workflow_analytics.py`, `auth.py`, `mobility_context.py`, `suppliers.py`, `policy_canonical.py`
2. For each state-changing endpoint (POST/PATCH/PUT/DELETE), add `insert_audit_log()` call
3. Priority order: `cases.py` (PATCH case, POST create) → `admin.py` (7 state-changing endpoints) → `auth.py` → rest
4. Verify tenant isolation: confirm every query in `cases.py` and `admin.py` is scoped to `organization_id`

**Track B — Quick wins T1.7–T1.11 (ship during spike week)**  
*These are independent. Don't queue them after spikes.*

- **T1.7 Paused status**: Add `PAUSED` to `CaseStatus` enum in `models.py` + migration. ~1 day.
- **T1.9 Hide RFQ**: Search frontend for RFQ component, gate behind feature flag or remove. ~half day.
- **T1.10 Dev tooling strip**: Search for debug/dev-only flags in frontend + backend. ~half day.
- **T1.11 UUID cleanup**: Grep for numeric IDs in URL patterns, replace with UUIDs. ~1 day.
- **T1.8 Command Center simplify**: `HrCommandCenter` is routed and exists — audit what it renders, identify noise to remove. ~1 day.

#### Week 2-3 — T1.5 v1: Wire the Estimate Review

*The skeleton is 80% built. What's missing is the wiring.*

**What exists:**
- `ServicesEstimate.tsx` — page shell, navigation, empty state
- `PackageSummary` — renders recommendations vs policy caps (`CATEGORY_TO_CAP`: housing/schools/movers), calls `convertUsdToDisplay()` and `formatEstimationFromUsd()`
- `servicesCurrency.ts` — 10-currency conversion, hardcoded indicative rates (labelled as "indicative planning values, not live FX" — correct for MVP)
- `useServicesFlow` context — `recommendations`, `shortlist`, `displayCurrency`

**What's missing for v1:**
1. HR-side view: HR manager sees the same estimate their employee sees, with override capability
2. Policy cap display completeness: `CATEGORY_TO_CAP` covers housing/schools/movers — verify transport, storage, language integration caps are also wired or explicitly labelled "not capped"
3. Subtotal vs cap delta: show clearly whether the shortlist is within or over the HR policy cap per category

**What to defer to v2 (after T1.3 + T1.4):**
- ExceptionRequest workflow (employee requests exception when over cap)
- Tenant-configurable cap thresholds (HR can adjust cap percentages)
- Live FX rates (current hardcoded rates are fine for MVP demo)

Claude Code starter prompt:
```
Read these files in full:
- frontend/src/pages/services/ServicesEstimate.tsx
- frontend/src/features/recommendations/PackageSummary.tsx
- frontend/src/features/services/servicesCurrency.ts
- frontend/src/features/services/ServicesFlowContext.tsx (find it)

Step 1: Map what ServicesEstimate already renders. What's working, what's 
placeholder, what's missing.

Step 2: In PackageSummary, identify which policy cap categories are wired

language_integration wired or missing?

Step 3: Add an HR-side view: when the viewing user is HR (check role), 
show the same estimate with an "Override cap" action stub (can be a 
disabled button for now — just needs to exist for the demo).

Step 4: Make the per-category delta explicit: for each category, show 
"Shortlist: €X / Cap: €Y / Status: within|over".

Don't touch FX rates or ExceptionRequest — those are v2.
```

#### Week 3 — T1.6: Audit EmployeeJourney depth

`EmployeeJourney.tsx` is a real page with a 5-step flow. Steps 3 ("Review budget vs policy") and 4 ("Request quotes — Soon") point directly at Estimate Review and ExceptionRequest.

What to do:
1. Load the page in staging, walk through all 5 steps
2. Identify what renders vs what's placeholder
3. Ship whatever polish is needed to make steps 1-3 demo-ready (step 4 stays "Soon" — that's T1.3 territory)

This is likely a 2-3 day polish task, not a week-long build.

### Sprint 2 — Weeks 4-10: Layer in Depth

**Goal:** Full Tier 1 critical path — ExceptionRequest workflow, T1.5 v2, remaining audit coverage, Spike 3 result actioned.

#### Weeks 4-5 — Spikes 3, 4, 1 (parallel, mostly analysis)

Run these now — spike results feed Tier 2 scope decisions.

**Spike 3 (contract_type / move_type):** No `contract_type` found in migrations yet — likely absent. If confirmed absent, T2.1 scope is a pure build. Start the design now so it doesn't surprise you.

**Spike 4 (spouse/family signal):** Half-day verification.

**Spike 1 (destination depth):** Content team work. Run in parallel with engineering.

#### Weeks 4-6 — T1.3: ExceptionRequest

This is the one real build in Tier 1. The `EXCEPTION` status exists as an enum value in `models.py` and `schemas.py` — nothing else is built.

What to build:
1. `exception_requests` table (Supabase migration): `id`, `case_id`, `organization_id`, `category`, `requested_amount`, `cap_amount`, `currency`, `reason`, `status` (pending/approved/rejected), `hr_note`, `created_at`, `resolved_at`
2. FastAPI router: `POST /api/cases/{case_id}/exceptions`, `GET /api/cases/{case_id}/exceptions`, `PATCH /api/cases/{case_id}/exceptions/{id}`
3. Employee UI: "Request exception" button in `PackageSummary` when category is over cap
4. HR UI: exception queue in `HrCommandCenter` or `HrAssignmentReview`
5. Every endpoint writes to `audit_log` (non-negotiable)

#### Weeks 5-6 — T1.4: FX backend wiring

Frontend FX is done. What's missing:
- Backend doesn't validate or normalise currency on ingest — hardcoded USD assumption in recommendation datasets
- Policy caps stored in USD nominal — need `display_currency` param on the estimate endpoint so HR and employee see the same number

This is 1-2 weeks of backend work, not 3-4. The hard part (frontend display layer) is already done.

#### Weeks 7-9 — T1.5 v2: Full Estimate Review

With T1.3 and T1.4 done, wire in:
1. ExceptionRequest: "Request exception" button live in PackageSummary
2. Tenant-configurable thresholds: HR can adjust cap % per category in policy config
3. HR approval view: exception queue, approve/reject with note
4. `insert_audit_log` on every exception state change

Split into 2 PRs:
- PR1: ExceptionRequest integration into estimate screen
- PR2: Tenant-configurable thresholds + HR approval queue

#### Week 9-10 — Remaining audit coverage (T1.1 completion)

Finish adding `insert_audit_log` to the remaining 10 routers from Sprint 1 Track A.
Add CI test: for every POST/PATCH/PUT/DELETE endpoint, assert audit_log row was written.
