## What ReloPass Does Well

### 1. Policy management that's actually structured

Most mobility platforms treat your policy as a Word document attachment. ReloPass treats it as data.

- **Two ways to start:** upload your existing PDF or DOCX policy and our AI extracts the caps and rules into a draft you review; OR start from a Conservative / Standard / Premium template with level-tiered caps for Entry / Manager / Director / VP / C-suite.
- **36+ benefit types structured** across 6 themes (Pre-assignment, Relocation assistance, Compensation & allowances, Family support & education, Leave & repatriation, Tax & payroll).
- **Per-benefit configuration:** amount, currency, unit (per_assignment / per_month / per_dependent), duration, approval-required.
- **3-column override pattern:** see the extracted baseline, your HR adjustment, and the effective policy side-by-side. Source-doc trace preserved.
- **Versioned drafts with publish workflow:** draft → mark for review → mark reviewed → publish. No more "which version of the policy is current?"
- **Live "Preview employee view"** so you can see exactly what your employees will see before publishing.

### 2. Cases as structured workflows, not folders of emails

Every case has a 5-phase plan with 16 default tasks (Pre-departure, Immigration, Logistics, Arrival, Post-arrival). Each task has an owner (HR / Employee / Both), a status, and dependencies. The Plan view answers "where are we?" in one screen — for both you and the employee.

- Family captured at intake: spouse name, "wants to work" flag, children with name + DOB.
- Passport auto-fill via OCR upload.
- Salary band (not exact value) for compliance-aware policy tier mapping.
- GDPR consent at submission.

### 3. Estimate Review — the screen your CFO will appreciate

When the employee selects movers, schools, housing, ReloPass computes:

- Each service vs the corresponding policy cap (€ amount, % under/over).
- Total package cost vs. total budget envelope.
- Out-of-policy spend the employee would cover personally — surfaced *before* commitment.
- Color-coded signals (green / yellow / red / grey for no-cap-defined / outline for excluded).
- Multi-year math shown explicitly (€15,000 × 1 child × 3 years = €45,000 cap).
- Currency conversions sourced from European Central Bank reference rates with the date stamped.

When a selection exceeds policy, the employee can: edit the selection, request a structured exception (routed to HR with full context), or proceed with personal cost acknowledged. No silent overruns.

### 4. Destination-aware recommendations

Recommendations are scored 0-100 against the employee's preferences and your policy:

- **Movers:** rated, lead-time aware, "Within budget" / "Above policy limit" tagged.
- **Schools:** quality scored, fees in the employee's preferred currency, application deadlines surfaced, commute analysis from office.
- **Neighborhoods:** lifestyle scoring (safety, green), monthly cost, commute time, "Limited availability" / "Waitlist may apply" flags.
- **Preferred vendors:** if you've worked with specific vendors, mark them "Preferred by your company" and they surface first for your employees.

### 5. Resources tailored to the destination and the employee

A welcome pack per destination and per employee profile (family-focused, permanent assignment, etc.) with curated content: visa essentials, banking, mobile, schools, neighborhoods, food, healthcare, transport, expat groups. Real local content (not generic "moving abroad" articles).

### 6. Audit-defensible case files

Every action — policy publish, case status change, document upload, package submission, exception request, HR approval — is logged with actor, timestamp, and tenant scoping. When your CFO or auditor asks "what happened on this case?", the answer is one click away.

## What ReloPass Doesn't Do (And Won't Pretend To)

- **We don't replace your immigration counsel.** Visa filings, work permit applications, immigration legal strategy — those stay with your existing firm. We track the case status; we don't do the filing.
- **We don't replace your tax provider.** Tax equalization computations, shadow payroll, US/Germany treaty analysis — those stay with KPMG / EY / your provider. We surface tax-related benefits as policy items; we don't compute tax structuring.
- **We don't replace your relocation services provider** for actual service delivery. Our recommendations engine helps you and your employees pick from your preferred vendors and competitive options. The actual mover, the actual school enrollment, the actual lease — those happen with the chosen vendor directly.
- **We're not the platform for 200+ cases per year.** If your mobility program is at that scale, you should be evaluating Topia or similar enterprise tooling — and we can integrate alongside if you have a specific use case where ReloPass adds value.

## Common Questions

**"How does pricing work?"**
SaaS subscription based on annual case volume + HR seats. Tiered (Starter / Professional / Enterprise depending on configuration). Pricing transparent on call — we don't play "request a quote" games. We're materially less expensive than the enterprise platforms because we're built for your scale, not theirs.

**"What happens to our existing vendor relationships?"**
They stay. ReloPass enables you to mark your preferred vendors so they surface first to your employees. Your RMC, your tax provider, your immigration firm continue exactly as today — ReloPass coordinates around them.

**"Can our policy live somewhere else?"**
Sure. Many customers keep their authoritative policy doc in their HRIS or document management system and use ReloPass as the operational layer that interprets the policy. We can extract from your existing PDF/DOCX or you can manage policy directly in ReloPass — your choice.

**"What's your data residency story?"**
Phase 1 deployment in [region]. Multi-region with EU-residency option in Phase 2. GDPR-compliant from day one with consent at intake, deletion-on-request, and data export.

**"Is your AI policy extraction reliable?"**
Match-strength scoring is visible — extraction confidence is exposed, and uncertain extractions require HR review before publication. We don't replace HR judgment; we accelerate the work.

**"How do you compare to [our existing setup]?"**
Honest answer: depends on what you're optimizing. If audit defensibility, employee experience, and HR-hour reduction matter — significant improvement. If you're running 200+ cases with mature mobility ops on Topia, we don't displace that; we may complement specific use cases.
