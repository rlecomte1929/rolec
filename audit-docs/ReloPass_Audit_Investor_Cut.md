## TL;DR

ReloPass is an AI-native global mobility platform targeting SME and mid-market international relocations (10-2000 employee customers running 5-50 cases/year), with a wedge built on two product anchors — **policy management as structured data** and **employee-facing budget reconciliation** — that meaningfully outperform legacy ops (Excel + RMC + immigration firm + tax provider).

**Verdict (calibrated):** 3-4x better than industry-average mobility ops for SME and mid-market international assignments. 2-3x better than the closest tech competitors (Localyze Business, Jobbatical) on the policy + budget reconciliation dimensions. **Complement, not displacement,** for tax-heavy cases and enterprise platforms (Topia post-Horizon).

**Strategic posture:** out-segment for MVP (SME + mid-market routine + French mid-market), complement for Phase 2 (Topia-class enterprise via integration), niche-attack only if signal warrants in 30+ months.

**Confidence:** Medium-high (70-75%) as a structural claim, conditional on five architectural verification spikes resolving and on customer-discovery validation. Direction is robust; absolute multiplier decimals are not.

**The single highest-leverage non-engineering investment to make right now:** customer-discovery interview wave (5-10 mid-market HR/mobility leads) to validate or refute the verdict claims. Audit is internal-evidence-grounded but field-untested.

## The Market and the Wedge

### The space

Global mobility — moving employees across borders for work — sits at the intersection of HR, immigration legal, tax, payroll, vendor management, and family logistics. A typical international assignment touches 5-12 vendors over 8-16 weeks, generates 200-500 emails, and costs €40-200k all-in. Industry-average failure rates: 10-20% of assignments end early, 15-25% of repats result in resignation within 2 years, 15% of assignments have audit gaps that surface during compliance reviews.

**The Total Addressable Market** is concentrated in three buyer segments:
- **SME (10-200 employees)** running 2-10 cases/year, mostly EU intra-EU corridors. Today: Excel + ad-hoc lawyer. ~250k+ companies in EU + UK alone.
- **Mid-market (200-2000 employees)** running 10-50 cases/year, mixed corridors. Today: relocation management company (RMC) + Excel + immigration firm + tax provider. ~25-30k companies in EU + UK alone.
- **Enterprise (2000+ employees)** running 50-500+ cases/year. Today: Topia / Equus / AssignmentPro + multi-vendor stack. ~3-5k companies globally.

### Where the wedge is

ReloPass targets **SME + mid-market routine** (200-2000 employees running 5-50 international cases/year, no significant US/heavy-tax mix, EU/Asia corridor focus). This is the segment where:

- Legacy ops costs the most relative to case value (50-90 HR hours per case on a €60-120k case).
- Audit defensibility is weakest (M10 stakeholder confidence 2-3 out of 5 typical).
- Existing tech alternatives are mid-priced and feature-thin (Localyze Business, Jobbatical) or wrong-shaped (Deel Mobility as a feature of an EOR, requiring full payroll migration).
- AI-native policy and budget reconciliation produces decisive operational and trust gains.

**The verdict-in-this-segment:** 3-4x improvement over legacy on operational metrics; 2-3x improvement over closest tech competitor on the policy + budget axis specifically; M10 stakeholder confidence 3 → 4 (the decisive PMF unlock that converts operational wins into purchase decisions).

### Where ReloPass is bounded (honest)

- **US/heavy-tax cases:** 80% of variance lives in tax structuring + immigration legal — both outside ReloPass MVP scope. ReloPass adds employee experience and case visibility but does not move M10 in this segment. Position as complement to existing Big-4 + immigration firm setup.
- **Enterprise (2000+):** Topia + RMC + Big-4 already deliver M10=4. Topia launched Horizon (agentic AI platform) on April 13, 2026, raising the displacement bar further. ReloPass cannot displace at this scale; Phase 2 complement-positioning is the long-term path.
- **Domestic-only (any segment):** below CFO awareness; small per-case savings. Operational gains are real but the buyer trust ceiling holds because there's no consumer of the data at HR program level.

## Strategic Positioning — The Topia-Threat Deliverable

April 13, 2026 was the most significant competitive event in the audit window. **Topia launched Horizon** — described as the first agentic AI platform purpose-built for global mobility — featuring AI Policy Builder (natural-language → audit-ready policies), Cost Modeling & Scenario Planning, Real-Time Compliance Intelligence, and Employee-First Experience. Topia is the enterprise-tier reference customer for global mobility (159 employees, $135M raised, Bow River Capital ownership, customers including Schneider Electric, Dell, Veolia, Equinor, AXA).

**The category bet ReloPass made — "AI-native mobility platform with policy + cost + employee experience" — is now the same bet Topia is making with Horizon.** This is simultaneously the most validating and most threatening competitive event in the audit window.

The strategic response is three-phased:

**Phase 1 (now → 12 months): Out-segment.** Do not pitch enterprise. Focus exclusively on SME + mid-market routine + French mid-market. Position as *"the AI-native mobility platform for companies running 5-50 international cases per year"* — explicitly differentiated from Topia by scale, not by capability.

**Phase 2 (12-30 months): Complement.** Build Topia One export integration. Pitch ReloPass as the *"employee-experience and SME-historical layer"* for enterprises whose mobility teams use Topia operationally. This is a second sale, not a competitive sale — sold to the same enterprise mobility VPs who already chose Topia.

**Phase 3 (30+ months): Niche-attack consideration.** Reactive, not proactive. If Topia downmarket motion happens (Horizon priced to compete in mid-market), revisit. If customer base graduates beyond Phase 1 segments and complement story doesn't hold, consider niche-attack on dimensions Topia is bad at (dual-career, permanent transfers, France-specific, non-Topia HRIS).

**What this position protects against:** spreading thin to compete with Topia (the textbook failure mode for sub-scale tech competing with $135M-funded incumbents). Discipline says *no, we're not building enterprise features for MVP because we're not chasing that customer.*

## Strengths

**S1 — Policy management is best-in-class for the wedge segment.** 36+ benefit types structured, 6 themes, draft/live versioning, 3-column override pattern (extracted baseline → HR adjustment → effective policy), match-strength scoring, two authoring paths (template + AI-extraction), per-benefit configuration (amount, currency, unit, duration, approval-required), level-tiered caps (Entry/Manager/Director/VP/C-suite). Tied with Topia Horizon at category-leading depth; meaningfully ahead of Localyze, Deel, Jobbatical.

**S2 — Case lifecycle with structured task graph.** 5-phase plan (Pre-departure, Immigration, Logistics, Arrival, Post-arrival), 16 tasks default, dependencies first-class, owner per task, priority flags. Solves "no single source of truth" universally across all 8 audit scenarios.

**S3 — Family/dependent capture with dual-career signal at intake.** Spouse "wants to work" flag at structured-data level. Most platforms ignore family or treat as text fields. 30-40% of failed assignments are due to spouse-employment issues; this signal is uncommon and material.

**S4 — Singapore destination depth as proof-of-concept.** 10 movers, 10 schools, 10 neighborhoods, 12+ resource categories, real Singapore vendor names, EP-specific watchouts. Pattern proven; expansion to 2-3 priority corridors is committed.

**S5 — Estimate Review (post-buildout) as the value-prop screen.** Color signaling (green ≤80% / yellow 80-100% / red >100%), per-service multiplier transparency, ECB FX with date stamping, exception flow integration. The single screen that turns abstract policy into concrete employee-facing budget guardrails. Tied with Topia Horizon Cost Modeling at category-leading specification.

**S6 — Architectural readiness for AI-native positioning.** Match-strength scoring on policy extraction, existing audit prompt suite for tenant isolation, recommendations engine with structured scoring, passport OCR. Foundational AI capabilities exist to claim AI-native positioning at par with the category leader.

## Methodology and Limitations

### Method

15 sequential tasks with stop-and-greenlight reviews. Reference workflow synthesized from NBIM Mobility Overview interview deck (9 internal + 5 external stakeholder interviews) + general mobility-ops literature. ReloPass product observation via 29 screenshots covering all major surfaces. Architecture inferred from screenshots + SRS document + project audit prompts (no codebase access). Competitive landscape via web search across 5 competitors with April 2026 evidence. Verdict calibrated against 8 representative scenarios across SME / mid-market / enterprise × LTA / STA / permanent-transfer / repat / domestic.

### Limitations

- **No codebase access.** Architecture is inferred (capability matrix tagged with confidence levels). Effort estimates ±50%.
- **No customer interviews.** Verdict is structurally robust but field-untested. Mitigated by R9 (discovery wave).
- **No live competitor product evaluation.** Competitor scoring from public materials, not hands-on demos.
- **Synthesized scoring throughout.** Every multiplier, every M10 score, every F-code mitigation percentage is calibrated, not measured. Direction is robust; absolute decimals are not.
- **Topia Horizon launched 2 weeks ago.** Capability claims not field-tested. Even at 70% of marketed capability, competitive shape unchanged.
- **Pricing comparison impossible.** None of the 5 competitors publish prices.
- **Domestic verdict (S8) and French wedge** lowest scoring confidence. Conditional on option (b) shipping clean AND Anywr window staying open.

## Capital Implications (Context)

This is a product audit, not a fundraising plan. But the audit surfaces capital implications worth flagging:

- **Phase 1 + 1.5 represent ~37-52 weeks of focused engineering work.** Parallelizable to ~21-29 wall-clock weeks with 2-3 engineers + content/research support.

- **Destination data scaling** is the biggest hidden ongoing cost. Per-corridor depth is 3-4 weeks of mobility-domain content per destination. A part-time mobility-domain hire (~30-50% FTE) becomes appropriate around Phase 1.5.
