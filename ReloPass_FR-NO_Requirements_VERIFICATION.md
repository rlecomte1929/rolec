## Non-obvious items (the demo)

### Item 1 — skattekort before first payroll (50% withholding) — ✅ CONFIRMED
- **Official quote:** *"Without a tax deduction card, the employer must deduct 50 percent tax."*
- **Source:** https://www.skatteetaten.no/en/business-and-organisation/foreign/employer/tax-deduction-cards/
- The 50% figure is current and exact; it is the **employer** who retrieves the card and applies the 50% deduction.
- **Change:** none required. Optional: your "before your first salary" is a fair paraphrase; the source frames it as "no card on file → 50% deduction."

### Item 2 — D-number (< 6 months) — ✅ CONFIRMED (1 softening)
- **Official quote:** *"A D number is a temporary identification number that you receive from the Norwegian Tax Administration when you … do not meet the criteria to being assigned a national identity number."* + *"The tax deduction card will not be issued until the worker has been to an ID check."*
- **Sources:** https://www.udi.no/en/word-definitions/d-number/ · https://www.skatteetaten.no/en/business-and-organisation/foreign/employer/tax-deduction-cards/
- Confirmed: issued by Skatteetaten, in the tax-card process, with an in-person ID check.
- **Change:** soften **"requires an in-person appointment"** → **"typically requires an in-person appointment"** (narrow exemptions exist: continental-shelf-only workers, seafarers on NO-registered ships).

### Item 3 — Police / EEA registration (> 3 months, within 3 months) — ✅ CONFIRMED
- **Official quotes:** *"EU/EEA nationals can move to Norway and start working right away, but they must register with the police no later than three months after arriving."* · *"If you are an EU/EEA national who are going to work and live in Norway for more than three months, you have to register."* · *"Registration is free."*
- **Sources:** https://www.udi.no/en/word-definitions/employers-employing-someone-who-is-an-eueea-national-/ · https://www.udi.no/en/want-to-apply/residence-under-the-eueeu-regulations/employee-who-is-an-eueea-national/
- Confirmed: start work immediately, >3-month trigger, within-3-months deadline, free, employment contract required.
- **Change:** none required. Optional (strong for the demo): add that registration needs an **in-person police appointment (ID check)** and **SUA Oslo has long waiting times** — so "within 3 months" is the legal rule but appointment availability is a real practical constraint (per the politiet SUA FAQ). This is *exactly* the kind of "week-seven ambush" your product exists to surface.

### Item 4 — A1 vs folketrygden (social security) — ✅ CONFIRMED + **1 substantive addition**
- **Official quotes:** NAV: *"If you are a citizen of an EU/EEA-country and come to work in Norway you automatically become a member of the Norwegian National Insurance Scheme from your first day of work."* CLEISS (France): the A1 attests French social-security law applies for the whole posting; French contributions continue.
- **Sources:** https://www.nav.no/en/home/rules-and-regulations/relatert-informasjon/coming-from-an-eu-eea-country-to-work-in-norway · https://www.cleiss.fr/particuliers/venir/travailler/detachement/ue883.html · https://www.skatteetaten.no/en/person/foreign/are-you-intending-to-work-in-norway/the-tax-return/are-you-exempt-from-the-requirement-to-pay-national-insurance-contributions-in-norway/
- Confirmed: EU coordination rules **do** apply to Norway (via the EEA Agreement); a genuinely **posted** French worker can use a France-issued A1 to stay in the French system and be exempt from Norwegian contributions.
- **ADD (your draft omits this):** the posting exemption has a **standard limit of 24 months** (Art. 12 of Reg. 883/2004). Beyond that, an **Article 16 exception agreement** between France and Norway can extend it. Also: A1 applies only to a **genuine posting** (French employer sends you to keep working for them) — a French national **hired locally** in Norway is **not** posted and pays folketrygden.
- **Flag before quoting a hard number:** confirm the exact Art. 16 extension ceiling for the FR–Norway corridor with URSSAF/CLEISS before stating a specific figure (commonly cited ~5 years, not yet corridor-confirmed).

## Baseline items

### Item 5 — National identity number / fødselsnummer (≥ 6 months) — ✅ CONFIRMED (1 softening)
- **Official quote:** *"If you intend to stay in Norway for at least six months consecutively or more permanently, you must report a move to Norway."*
- **Source:** https://www.skatteetaten.no/en/person/national-registry/identitetsnummer-og-elektronisk-id/fodselsnummer/ · https://www.udi.no/en/word-definitions/national-identity-number/
- **Change:** soften **"replaces any D-number"** → **"supersedes any D-number"** (official sources don't use "replaces"; functionally the fødselsnummer takes over, but stay within their wording).

### Item 6 — Entry document: EEA national ID card accepted — ✅ CONFIRMED (1 softening)
- **Official quote:** *"…you have the right to stay in Norway as an EU/EEA national if you are an employee … and you have a valid identity card or passport."*
- **Source:** https://www.udi.no/en/want-to-apply/residence-under-the-eueeu-regulations/
- **Change:** the sources frame this as the document proving the **right to stay/register**, not literally "entry." Soften **"acceptable for entry"** → **"a valid identity/travel document"** to match UDI's framing.

### Item 7 — Employment contract / certificate — ✅ CONFIRMED
- **Official quote:** *"you must provide your worker with an employment certificate … or an employment contract with the same information … The employee must present the certificate or contract to the police when registering."*
- **Source:** https://www.udi.no/en/word-definitions/employers-employing-someone-who-is-an-eueea-national-/
- **Change:** none. Your "employment contract/certificate" phrasing is exactly right (either is accepted).
